Hey DibsListing generator

1 Photos

In Blogger tap + NEW POST, upload your photos, switch to HTML view, select all, copy, and paste it here. (Do not close the Blogger tab.)

2 Item

This becomes the post title, for example: Sonic the Hedgehog 2.5-Inch Action Figure ₱1,200

3 Details

One detail per line. Use a colon to make the first part bold, like Figures: Sonic and Tails.

4 Labels

The shop uses labels to sort items, show badges, and set stock and shipping. Choose here and copy the result into Blogger's Labels box.

Leave blank for no limit.

5 Auction settings

Customers will place bids on the item page.

24 = 1 day, 168 = 7 days.
Leave blank. The shop uses the post's own ID automatically.

5 Your contact

Customers will message you about your items, and orders for your items go to your WhatsApp. Leave both blank to use the shop's contact. We remember this on this device.

Before you post

    A. Post title

    B. Labels

    Paste into the Labels box in Blogger. Extra labels you may use yourself: Sold when it sells.

    C. Post HTML

    How to post

    1. In Blogger, open your new post and switch to HTML view.
    2. Paste A into the title and C into the post area.
    3. Paste B into the Labels box.
    4. Save it as a Draft.

    Do not publish. Wait for the admin to publish the listing.
